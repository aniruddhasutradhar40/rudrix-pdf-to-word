    Notes for review

      Which mode should I choose?

      Editable Word

      Text becomes normal Word paragraphs and headings, tables become Word tables, and pictures are placed inline. Page size and margins follow the PDF. Complex layouts (text boxes, overlapping items, vector charts) are simplified.

      Maximum Layout Preservation

      Uses a layout-reconstruction engine to keep positions, columns, fonts, tables and images close to the original page. The result is closer to the PDF but can contain positioned text blocks that are harder to edit.

      What to expect

      No PDF converter can promise identical formatting for every file, because PDF stores drawing instructions, not a flowing document. Rudrix compares the result against your PDF and tells you what it could and could not verify. Always glance through the Word file before sending it on.
